# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used throughout the Enterprise E-Commerce Analytics Platform.

The KPI Framework establishes standardized metric definitions to ensure consistency across SQL queries, Python transformations, DuckDB models, exploratory analysis, dashboards, and executive reporting.

---

# 2. KPI Design Principles

All KPIs must satisfy the following principles:

* Clearly defined business meaning.
* Single source of truth.
* Reproducible calculations.
* Consistent analytical grain.
* Traceable to business questions.
* Suitable for executive reporting.
* Based only on validated historical data.

# 4. KPI Definitions

## KPI-01 — Total Revenue

**Business Definition**

Total monetary value generated from completed customer purchases.

**Business Question**

How much revenue has the marketplace generated?

**Primary Stakeholder**

Executive Board, Finance Manager

**Analytical Grain**

Marketplace

**Primary Dimensions**

* Month
* Year
* Category
* Seller
* State

**Formula**

To be finalized after Data Understanding (Sprint 2).

**Notes**

The project will determine the official revenue definition after validating the available transaction fields.

---

## KPI-02 — Total Orders

**Business Definition**

Total number of completed customer orders included in the analytical scope.

**Business Question**

How many orders were successfully completed?

**Primary Stakeholder**

Executive Board

**Analytical Grain**

Order

**Primary Dimensions**

* Date
* Seller
* Customer
* Category

---

## KPI-03 — Total Customers

**Business Definition**

Number of unique customers included in the analysis.

**Business Question**

How many customers purchased from the marketplace?

**Primary Stakeholder**

Marketing Manager

---

## KPI-04 — Average Order Value (AOV)

**Business Definition**

Average monetary value per completed order.

**Business Question**

What is the average value generated by each order?

**Primary Stakeholder**

Executive Board

---

## KPI-05 — Monthly Revenue Growth

Measures month-over-month revenue growth.

---

## KPI-06 — Repeat Customer Rate

Measures the proportion of customers making more than one purchase.

---

## KPI-07 — Best Selling Products

Ranks products by sales volume and sales value.

---

## KPI-08 — Category Revenue

Measures revenue contribution by product category.

---

## KPI-09 — Seller Performance

Evaluates seller contribution through revenue, order volume, and customer ratings.

---

## KPI-10 — Average Delivery Time

Measures the average duration between purchase approval and delivery.

---

## KPI-11 — Late Delivery Rate

Measures the percentage of delivered orders arriving after the estimated delivery date.

---

## KPI-12 — Average Review Score

Measures overall customer satisfaction based on review scores.

# 6. KPI Governance

All KPI calculations must follow these governance rules:

* KPI definitions cannot be changed without updating this document.
* Every SQL script must reference the corresponding KPI.
* Every dashboard metric must map to a KPI in this framework.
* Business recommendations must explicitly reference one or more KPIs.
* Any assumptions introduced during analysis must be documented.

---

# 7. KPI Traceability

Each KPI will be linked to:

* Business Question
* Source Tables
* Required Fields
* SQL Transformation
* DuckDB Model
* Polars Transformation
* EDA Notebook
* Power BI Dashboard
* Executive Report

This ensures full traceability from business objectives to technical implementation.

---

# 8. KPI Validation Strategy

Formal validation of formulas will occur during Sprint 2 (Data Understanding) after the source datasets have been profiled.

Validation activities include:

* Confirming source columns.
* Identifying inclusion and exclusion rules.
* Resolving duplicate records.
* Assessing data completeness.
* Verifying aggregation logic.

Only after validation will KPI formulas be considered production-ready.
