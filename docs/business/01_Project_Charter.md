# Project Charter

## Project Name

Enterprise E-Commerce Analytics Platform

---

## Project Overview

This project aims to build an end-to-end analytics platform for an e-commerce business using modern analytics engineering tools.

The platform transforms raw transactional CSV files into business insights through a structured analytics workflow consisting of data ingestion, validation, transformation, exploratory analysis, visualization, and executive dashboards.

---

## Business Problem

E-commerce companies generate large volumes of transactional data every day. Although the data is available, business teams often struggle to answer important questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which customers contribute the most value?
- What factors influence repeat purchases?
- Which product categories are growing?
- Where are operational bottlenecks occurring?

Without a structured analytics platform, decision-making becomes slower and less reliable.

---

## Project Objective

Design and implement an end-to-end analytics solution that enables stakeholders to monitor business performance and make data-driven decisions.
